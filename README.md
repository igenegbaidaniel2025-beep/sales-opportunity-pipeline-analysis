Sales Opportunity & Pipeline Analysis

Overview

A sales opportunity analysis project built in Excel to evaluate sales performance, pipeline activity, product performance, and potential revenue.

Business Objective

The goal was to turn sales opportunity data into clear reports that help management understand the sales pipeline, identify opportunities, and evaluate performance across managers, products, regions, and accounts.

Analysis Covered

Sales Manager Report

Analyzed performance across sales managers, including:

- Sales opportunities
- Closed opportunities
- Close rate
- Sales value
- Close value
- Weeks to close

Sales Pipeline Analysis

Examined:

- Deal stages
- Engaged vs. closed opportunities
- Pipeline performance
- Regional performance
- Account and sector distribution
- Potential revenue

Product Report

Analyzed product performance across:

- Product series
- Sales opportunities
- Sales value
- Close value
- Deal outcomes

Key Business Questions

- Which sales managers performed best?
- How strong is the current sales pipeline?
- Which products generate the most opportunity value?
- Which regions and sectors show the strongest opportunities?
- How long do opportunities take to close?
- What proportion of opportunities are successfully closed?

Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Data visualization
- Dashboard/report design

Business Relevance

The analysis provides management with a clearer view of sales performance and pipeline health, helping identify strong opportunities, evaluate sales teams, and support better revenue decisions.
